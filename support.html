FAMILIAR FACES · HELP

People who matter,
close at hand.

Photos, familiar names and personal introductions, ready to return to.

Use face packs for family and friends, a support team, recovery, a new setting or everyday connection. Use the app independently or with someone you support. You do not need to enter an age or diagnosis.

Create your first face pack

  1. Choose Create a face pack and create or enter your setup PIN.
  2. Name your pack. You can optionally add context and who prepared it.
  3. Add a person. Start with their name, then add a photo, relationship, written introduction or video.
  4. Choose Done to return to viewing. Tap a person to open their introduction.

You can add up to 24 people per pack. Only people you add are shown. Try Show me an example for a fictional example.

Make a personal introduction

Record a short clip in Camera, then select it in the app. Try 10–30 seconds: say your name, your relationship or role, and something familiar or reassuring. A written introduction can be used alongside a video or on its own.

Videos play when you choose Play introduction. Turn on Repeat video if you want a clip to repeat.

Share with someone else

Open your pack, choose Edit & share, enter your setup PIN and choose Share this face pack. The file includes its photos, names, written introductions and videos.

The recipient needs a compatible version of Familiar Faces. They can tap the file in Mail, Messages or Files, or use Open a shared pack. They review it before adding it. Once saved, the pack can be viewed offline.

Pack sharing is available in version 1.1.0. The original App Store version 1.0.0 does not open these shared packs.

Keep shared copies up to date

Changes are not automatic. Edit the original pack and share it again. When its identifier matches a saved pack, the recipient can confirm replacement using their device’s setup PIN. Replacement includes removing local edits or people not present in the incoming pack. The app warns if the incoming copy is the same age or older.

Agree who maintains the main copy when several people are involved. There is no automatic merging, live team editing or central access control. Shared files can be kept or forwarded and cannot be recalled.

A pack is too large to share

Completed shared files must be below 60 MB. The app compresses shared photo and video copies while preserving saved originals. If a complete video cannot fit, sharing reports an error. Try shorter clips or divide people into smaller packs; videos are never silently omitted.

Forgotten your setup PIN?

Choose Forgot PIN? and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. You can then choose a new PIN without removing your packs. The setup PIN helps prevent accidental edits; it does not encrypt shared files.

Photos or videos are missing

If the app reports a missing video, choose it again from Photos or ask the sender to resend the pack. Keep important original photos and videos backed up. If a save fails, check your device’s free storage; the previously saved pack stays unchanged.

Delete a pack

Open Edit & share and choose Delete this pack. Copies already sent to other people are not removed from their devices.

Privacy and accessibility

The app does not require an account. Packs are stored locally; share only with intended recipients and with permission from the people featured. Larger text, VoiceOver labels and written introductions support different ways of using the app. Read the privacy policy.
