FAMILIAR FACES

Privacy policy

Updated 19 September 2026 · Applies to version 1.1.0

Familiar Faces is a personal communication aid for photos, names and introductions. It can be used by an individual or by someone supporting them. It does not require an account, diagnosis or date of birth.

Information you choose to add

Face packs may contain names, relationships or roles, photos, written introductions, videos, optional context and a “prepared by” name. Packs also contain identifiers, revision numbers and dates used to recognise updates. This information is stored in the app on your device.

Information sent to the developer

The app has no developer-operated server, advertising SDK or third-party tracking SDK. Its code does not send face-pack content to the developer. Apple may provide its own store, crash or beta-testing information under your Apple settings and its privacy terms.

Sharing a pack

Sharing is your choice. A .familiarfaces file includes the pack’s names, context, photos, introductions and videos. The service you select in the share sheet handles delivery under its own terms. Only share with intended recipients, and obtain appropriate permission from the people whose information you include.

Recipients can retain, edit or forward their copies. The app does not apply its own encryption, expiry or remote revocation to exported files. Removing or editing your pack does not change previously shared copies. There is no automatic synchronisation.

Device storage and protection

Your setup PIN is stored in the iOS Keychain. It helps prevent accidental editing on the device and does not protect exported files. Resetting an existing PIN requires device authentication. The app uses Apple’s authentication interface and does not receive your biometric information or device passcode.

Device backups may include local app data depending on your Apple settings. Version 1.1.0 does not actively sync packs through CloudKit. Older versions may have stored data in your personal iCloud account; this update does not remove those earlier cloud copies.

Photos and videos

You choose media through Apple’s photo picker. Videos selected for use are prepared locally for playback and sharing. The app does not upload media for processing.

Deletion

Use Edit & share to delete individual packs from the app. Already-shared copies and device backups are separate and may need to be removed separately. An original local data file may be retained as a recovery copy during migration from older versions. Removing the app removes its local app container; manage any device backups and earlier iCloud data through your Apple settings.

Children and people receiving support

The app can be used with people of different ages and support needs. The person preparing a pack should include only information needed for its purpose, consider the preferences of the person using it, and agree who should receive a copy. A diagnosis or private care history is not required.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Daniel Waterhouse at danieljwaterhouse@hotmail.com. Please do not send setup PINs or private face packs.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. New collaboration or cloud features would require their own clear explanation before use.
